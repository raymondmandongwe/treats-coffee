Shipping & Delivery

How much does shipping cost?

Shipping is R99 nationwide. Orders over R600 qualify for free shipping — automatically applied at checkout.

When will my order be dispatched?

We dispatch on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays. Orders placed before 8am on a dispatch day go out the same day. All other orders go out on the next available dispatch day.

This schedule ensures your coffee is always roasted fresh — never sitting in a box over the weekend.

How long does delivery take?

Typically 2–3 business days after dispatch. Cape Town deliveries often arrive the next business day. Remote or outlying areas may take longer.

Do you ship outside South Africa?

Not currently. We ship to all provinces within South Africa. If you're based in a neighbouring country and keen to work something out, please contact us directly.

Can I track my order?

Yes. Once your order is dispatched, you'll receive a tracking number by email. You can use this on the courier's website to follow your delivery.

Ordering

Can I choose my grind size?

Yes. Select your preferred grind at checkout: Whole Bean, Fine (Espresso), Medium-Fine (AeroPress), Medium (Pour-over/Drip), or Coarse (French Press). We grind to order.

We recommend whole bean if you have a grinder — freshly ground coffee makes a noticeable difference.

What size bags do you sell?

Our standard bags are 250g. If you need larger quantities for business use, please see our Wholesale page.

Can I set up a subscription?

We're working on a subscription feature. In the meantime, email us and we can arrange a recurring order manually at a small discount.

How fresh is the coffee when it arrives?

Very fresh. We roast on dispatch days and only dispatch on those days. In most cases your coffee was roasted within 24–48 hours of shipping. We recommend resting espresso blends for 5–7 days after roasting, and filter coffee for 3–5 days, for the best flavour.

Grinding & Brewing

What grind should I use for my espresso machine?

Select Fine (Espresso) at checkout. If you have your own grinder, start fine and adjust coarser until you get an extraction of about 25–30 seconds for a 36–40g shot from 18g of coffee.

What's the best brewing method for the Ethiopian Yirgacheffe?

We love it as a pour-over or V60. The clarity of a paper-filtered brew lets all that floral, blueberry character shine. A French press also works beautifully if you like a heavier body. Avoid milk — it'll mask the fruit.

My espresso is coming out bitter / sour. What's wrong?

Bitter usually means over-extracted — grind slightly coarser, or shorten your brew time. Sour usually means under-extracted — grind slightly finer, or increase your brew time. If you're stuck, drop us an email with your setup details and we'll help you dial it in.

Wholesale

Is there a minimum order for wholesale?

No hard minimum. We work with businesses of all sizes, from small offices ordering 2kg a week to high-volume cafés ordering 20kg+. Send us an enquiry and we'll work out what makes sense for you.

Do you offer training for our baristas?

Yes. We offer on-site training for wholesale partners, covering machine calibration, grinder setup, milk technique, and coffee knowledge. This is included for new partners.

What areas do you deliver wholesale to?

We supply the Cape Town metropolitan area and surrounds primarily. We can courier further afield — please enquire for pricing and scheduling.

The Café

Do you take reservations?

We don't currently take reservations for individual tables. For groups of 8 or more, please contact us in advance and we'll do our best to accommodate you.

Is the café dog-friendly?

Dogs are welcome in our outdoor seating area. Please keep them leashed and well-behaved around other guests.

Do you have vegan / gluten-free options?

Yes. We offer oat and almond milk for all coffee drinks. Our kitchen team can advise on gluten-free options on the day — please ask your server.

Returns

What if I'm not happy with my coffee?

Please get in touch. We want every customer to enjoy their coffee. If something isn't right, we'll make it right — whether that's a replacement, a different product, or a refund.

My order arrived damaged. What should I do?

Send us an email with a photo of the damage and your order number. We'll send a replacement as soon as possible at no cost to you.
