The Beginning

From Passion to Roastery

Treat Coffee Roasters began as a simple obsession with a great cup. We wanted to understand every variable between the farm and the cup — and eventually, we wanted to control the most important one: the roast.

We set up our roastery in Durbanville, Cape Town, and started roasting small batches for friends, family, and a handful of local cafés. The response was immediate. People tasted the difference that freshness and care make.

Today we roast multiple days a week, supply businesses across the Cape, and welcome customers into our flagship café where you can taste our entire range and meet the team behind the coffee.

What We Stand For

Our Values

01

Freshness Above All

We roast to order. Every bag dispatched within days of roasting. Coffee is a perishable product and we treat it as one.

02

Traceability

We know where our beans come from. We share that information with you. Transparency is the foundation of trust.

03

Community

We're a Cape Town business. We buy locally where possible, support local cafés, and show up for our community.

04

Education

We want every customer — from curious beginner to seasoned barista — to understand and love what's in their cup.
